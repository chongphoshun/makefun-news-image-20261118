judge compares the images two at a time and picks the one that best sums up
that day's world news.

## The day's six stories

   Qeshm Island; Iran fires drones at Bahrain and Kuwait.
2. **Kerch Bridge falls** — Ukraine hits the bridge a second day; part of
   the road span collapses into the strait.
3. **Summit tomorrow in Shenzhen** — Xi, Putin and Trump meet at APEC; Xi
   hosts Putin first.
4. **Ebola surge in Congo** — Outbreak more than doubles: 11,000 cases,
   5,400 deaths.
5. **US loses measles-free status** — After 26 years; 5,400 cases, 10
   deaths.
6. **Russian jet crashes; 19 dead** — Military plane down in Siberia.

Sources: Al Jazeera, Reuters, AP — 18 Nov 2026.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` (1024×1024,
678 KB) to `/output/entry.png`. Run it locally with:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
